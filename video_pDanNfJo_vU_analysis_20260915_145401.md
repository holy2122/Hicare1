The video analysis of the hypertension management routine by Korean expert Dr. Kim Gyeong-tae is detailed below:

### **1. Exercise Routine and Intervals**
*   **Exercise Type:** Wall Squat (벽 스쿼트), categorized as an isometric exercise (등척성 운동).
*   **Exercise Duration:** Each set involves holding the squat position for **2 minutes**.
*   **Rest Duration:** A **2-minute rest** period is required between each exercise set.
*   **Total Routine Time:** The complete routine takes **16 minutes** (8 minutes of exercise and 8 minutes of rest).

### **2. Sets and Weekly Frequency**
*   **Number of Sets:** The routine consists of **4 sets** total.
*   **Weekly Frequency:** Recommended **3 times per week**, specifically using an "on-off" schedule (e.g., exercise one day, rest the next).

### **3. Posture and Safety Instructions**
*   **Posture:**
    *   Lean the entire back (buttocks, waist, and upper back) flat against the wall.
    *   Slide down the wall until the knees are ideally at a **90-degree angle**.
    *   Hands can be placed on the chest or rested on the thighs.
*   **Safety/Modifications:** Dr. Kim explicitly advises that if the 90-degree position causes knee pain or feels too burdensome, the user should only descend to a depth they can comfortably manage.

### **4. Physiological Mechanism**
*   **Contraction Phase:** During the 2-minute hold, the leg muscles contract, causing the blood vessels to constrict (수축).
*   **Dilation Phase:** During the 2-minute rest, the blood vessels dilate (확장) as blood flow increases.
*   **Effect:** This repetitive constriction and dilation improves the **elasticity (탄력)** of the blood vessels (described as making them "chewy" or "쫀득쫀득"). 
*   **Result:** Improved vessel elasticity reduces the resistance against which the heart must pump, thereby lowering and stabilizing blood pressure more effectively than aerobic running alone.
