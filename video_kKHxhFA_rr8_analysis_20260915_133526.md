This visual and audio analysis examines the exercise video titled "Easy Post-Meal Exercises to Lower Blood Sugar," featuring a fitness expert demonstrating chair-based movements.

### **Video Overview**
*   **Purpose:** To lower blood sugar levels post-meal and prevent diabetes complications.
*   **Main Equipment:** A stable indoor chair used for balance or seated support.
*   **Setting:** A living room environment.

### **Detailed Exercise Analysis**

#### **1. Quadriceps Stretch (허벅지 앞 스트레칭)**
*   **Order:** 1st
*   **Movements:** Stand with feet hip-width apart. Hold the chair back with one hand for balance. Bend one leg back and grasp the ankle/foot with the corresponding hand, pulling the heel toward the glutes.
*   **Duration:** 15 seconds per side.
*   **Safety/Setup:** Maintain an upright posture; the bent knee should point straight down and not drift forward.
*   **Tips:** The chair provides stability for those with poor balance.

#### **2. Calf Stretch (종아리 스트레칭)**
*   **Order:** 2nd
*   **Movements:** Stand facing the chair and grip the backrest with both hands. Step one leg far back (about double shoulder-width) keeping the heel pressed firmly into the floor. Bend the front knee slightly and lean forward.
*   **Duration:** 15 seconds per side.
*   **Safety/Setup:** Ensure the back leg is straight and the heel remains in contact with the ground to maximize the stretch.
*   **Tips:** Regular stretching of large muscle groups helps manage blood sugar.

#### **3. Squats (스쿼트)**
*   **Order:** 3rd
*   **Movements:** Stand facing the chair with hands on the backrest. Feet shoulder-width apart, toes angled outward by 15 degrees. Lower hips until thighs are roughly parallel to the floor, then stand back up.
*   **Repetitions:** 12 reps.
*   **Intensity/Tips:** If the intensity is too high, the narrator suggests reducing the number of repetitions. Focus on the hip and knee joints during the movement.

#### **4. Glute Extensions (둔근 운동)**
*   **Order:** 4th
*   **Movements:** Stand facing the chair, holding the backrest. Extend one leg straight back behind the body without bending the knee, then return to the start.
*   **Repetitions:** 12 reps per side.
*   **Contraindications/Safety:** Maintain core tension ("power in the abdomen") to prevent the lower back from arching, which could cause strain.
*   **Tips:** Focus on feeling the contraction in the gluteal muscles.

#### **5. Calf Raises (종아리 운동)**
*   **Order:** 5th
*   **Movements:** Stand facing the chair with hands on the backrest. Lift both heels as high as possible, hold briefly to feel tension in the calves, and lower them slowly.
*   **Repetitions:** 12 reps.
*   **Safety/Setup:** Move in a controlled manner to avoid sudden jolts to the ankles.
*   **Tips:** This exercise is specifically highlighted as being effective for preventing diabetes complications by improving lower-limb circulation.

#### **6. Hamstring Stretch (햄스트링 스트레칭)**
*   **Order:** 6th
*   **Movements:** Sit on the edge of the chair. Extend one leg forward, resting only the heel on the floor with toes pointed toward the ceiling. Keep the back straight and lean the torso forward over the extended leg.
*   **Duration:** 15 seconds per side.
*   **Safety/Setup:** Ensure the chair is stable. Keep the spine neutral (do not hunch) while leaning forward.
*   **Tips:** This concludes the routine by relaxing the posterior chain muscles.

*   **Intensity:** Low to moderate, suitable for beginners or elderly individuals.
*   **Frequency:** The narrator emphasizes that "consistent exercise" is crucial for effective blood sugar management.
*   **Safety Note:** While not explicitly mentioned, viewers should ensure their chair does not have wheels and is placed on a non-slip surface to prevent falls.
